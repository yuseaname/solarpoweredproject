Key takeaways

  • Panels are usually the largest hardware cost.
  • Batteries can double total cost for off-grid systems.
  • Balance-of-system parts add meaningful cost.

Typical cost ranges

ComponentTypical rangeNotes
Panels$0.40 - $1.20 per wattCost drops at higher wattage
Inverter$0.20 - $0.70 per wattDepends on type and features
Batteries$200 - $900 per kWhLi-ion usually higher upfront
Balance of system10% - 25% of totalWiring, mounts, breakers

What changes the price?

System size, battery choice, inverter type, mounting complexity, and local labor rates all affect totals.
