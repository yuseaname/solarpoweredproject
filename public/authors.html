Solar Powered Project publishes practical field guides designed to make independent power decisions clearer.

Solar Powered Project is an editorial publication focused on practical independent power questions. We publish guides about solar systems, batteries, wiring, costs, troubleshooting, and carefully framed off-grid experiments.

Bylines and reviewer notes are shown where they are available and accurate. Our editorial policy and methodology explain how we approach sources, updates, and corrections.
