About Solar Guide

SolarPoweredProject.com publishes clear, practical solar and off-grid guides for homeowners, RVers, and DIY learners.

We are independent and educational. We do not sell solar hardware or installation services.

Our mission

We focus on practical decision-making: system sizing, costs, safety, and real-world performance. The goal is to help you avoid common mistakes and plan realistically before spending money.

Editorial standards

Content is written to be understandable without sacrificing accuracy. We prioritize clear definitions, realistic assumptions, and links to supporting guides. See our editorial policy for more detail.

Advertising and independence

The site is ad-supported. Ads help fund ongoing updates and new guides. Advertising partners do not control editorial decisions.
