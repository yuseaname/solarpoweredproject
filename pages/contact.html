Contact Solar Guide

Questions, corrections, or content feedback are welcome.

We provide general education, not individualized engineering or installation advice.

Key takeaways

  • Email is the best way to reach us.
  • Include the page URL and a short description of the issue.
  • We review messages and respond when we can, but response times vary.
  • We cannot provide custom system designs or safety inspections.

Email

Reach us at contact@solarpoweredproject.com.

If your question relates to privacy or data, please mention that in the subject line. You can also review our privacy policy and terms of use.

What to include

  • Page URL or topic you are referencing.
  • What seems incorrect, unclear, or outdated.
  • Any sources or data that support your note.
  • Your general location if the question involves codes or permitting.

What we can help with

  • Clarifying a concept in an existing guide.
  • Correcting a typo, calculation, or wording issue.
  • Suggesting a topic or comparison you want to see.
  • Explaining how to use our calculators.

What we cannot provide

  • Custom system design, wiring diagrams, or load calculations for your home.
  • On-site inspections or troubleshooting of installed systems.
  • Legal or engineering advice about local electrical code compliance.

For safety-critical work, consult a licensed electrician or installer. If you are new to solar planning, the system sizing guide is a good starting point.

Response expectations

We read every message and aim to respond when time allows. We are a small editorial operation, so responses may take a few business days or longer during high volume. If your issue is urgent or safety-related, please contact a local professional directly.

If you do not receive a response, feel free to send a short follow-up with the original subject line.

Suggested topics

If you are requesting a new guide, tell us what decision you are trying to make and what is missing from existing pages. We prioritize topics that help readers size systems, compare components, and understand real-world limitations.

FAQ

Can you review my system design?

No. We cannot review or approve individual designs. Use our guides for general education only.

Do you accept corrections or additional sources?

Yes. Please include the page URL and source links so we can verify.

Can I request a new calculator?

Yes. Send a short note about the use case and what inputs you want to estimate.

Do you have a phone number?

No. Email is the best way to reach us.
