Solar Powered Project is an independent, reader-supported publication about practical independent power: solar systems, batteries, wiring, costs, and off-grid builds.

## Why a publication byline

We publish under the publication name rather than individual bylines because the guides here are built, checked, and maintained as a shared process — calculations reviewed against manufacturer documentation and standards, cost ranges checked against public data, and corrections logged publicly — rather than as the personal opinion of one writer. We do not present a named "expert" reviewer unless that person's review actually happened. That is a deliberate honesty choice: an anonymous-byline site pretending to be a named-expert site would be worse than neither.

## What we actually do

- **Build answers from the math out.** Sizing, runtime, payback, and wiring guides show their arithmetic and state their assumptions. If a number matters, you can check it.
- **Read primary sources.** Manufacturer spec sheets, the NEC where wiring is involved, government data (EIA, IRS, state program pages) for costs and incentives. Where we state a range, we say it's a range.
- **Log our corrections.** Mistakes get fixed and recorded on the [corrections page](/corrections.html) — including a September 2026 pass that removed stale federal tax-credit claims from several cost guides.
- **Update in place.** Time-sensitive pages say their scope; policy changes (like the December 31, 2025 expiry of the federal residential clean-energy credit) get applied across the site, not just on new pages.

## What we do not claim

- We do not run a test lab, and we do not claim hands-on testing we have not done. Where a page mentions specific equipment, it is because the specs and the math point there — not because a bench test told us so. When we do document a real build or measurement in the [Project Lab](/diy-off-grid-energy.html), the measurements and their limits are stated in that article.
- We do not accept payment for coverage, and brands do not see or approve anything before publication.
- We are not electricians, and nothing here is a substitute for a licensed professional for code work. Safety-critical steps say so.

## How the site is funded

Reader-supported: we earn affiliate commissions (mainly Amazon Associates) when you buy through links, at no extra cost to you. See the [affiliate disclosure](/pages/affiliate-disclosure.html) and [how we pick what we link](/pages/how-we-recommend.html).
