Physics-first experiments in alternative generation. Every article leads with the arithmetic — what a device of a given size can actually produce — before any build list, because most DIY generation ideas die on the math, not the workshop. These are planning guides with realistic estimates and stated limits, not documented test-lab results; when we later document a real build with measurements, the article will say so explicitly.
