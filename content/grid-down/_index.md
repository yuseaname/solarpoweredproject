Power systems built for the day the grid can't help you — planned with arithmetic, not adrenaline. Every article in this section leads with what the physics actually delivers, what a tested component is worth versus a pile of mystery hardware, and what to do first when watt-hours are scarce. These are engineering planning guides: we state what is established physics, what is manufacturer specification, and what we have not tested. Nothing here is survivalist theater.
