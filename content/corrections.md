Accuracy matters when a reader is planning an electrical system, comparing costs, or making a safety decision.

To report a potential error, include the page URL, the statement in question, and any relevant source or context through our [contact page](/pages/contact.html). We review reports and update content when a correction or clarification is warranted.

Updates may clarify assumptions, repair links, refresh time-sensitive information, or correct factual errors. We do not represent an article as reviewed by a named specialist unless that review is real and disclosed.
