## Our editorial goal

We publish practical field guides that help readers understand independent power systems and make better next decisions. Clear explanations are useful only when their assumptions and limits are also clear.

## Accuracy and sources

For claims that materially affect safety, policy, cost, performance, or equipment choices, we aim to prioritize primary or authoritative references such as manufacturer documentation, government agencies, utilities, standards bodies, and established research organizations. Sources and context should be visible where they matter—not implied by a general statement.

## Updates and dated information

Costs, incentives, technology, and equipment specifications change. We may update an article to clarify assumptions, repair links, correct errors, or refresh time-sensitive material. A publication or review date does not mean a claim was independently tested unless that testing is specifically described.

## Advertising independence

Solar Powered Project is ad-supported. Advertising partners do not direct editorial coverage or determine conclusions. Advertisements are visually separated from editorial navigation and primary interactive controls.

## Product, cost, and comparison content

Comparisons and cost estimates should state their scope, assumptions, and relevant variability. They are planning information, not a guarantee of price, performance, compatibility, or outcome.

## Electrical and safety boundaries

Electrical systems can present serious safety and code issues. We aim to highlight relevant constraints, but published guidance is not a substitute for a qualified professional, applicable code requirements, equipment documentation, or site-specific design review.

## Corrections

Readers can report a potential error, missing context, outdated guidance, or broken link through our [contact page](contact.html). See [Corrections & Updates](/corrections.html) for the process.
