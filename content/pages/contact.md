## Contact

Reach us at [contact@solarpoweredproject.com](mailto:contact@solarpoweredproject.com).

## For corrections or technical feedback

Please include:

- the page URL or topic;
- the statement that seems incorrect, incomplete, or unclear;
- any useful source, specification, or context;
- whether the issue concerns safety, pricing, policy, a calculation, or a broken link.

We review reports through the process described on our [Corrections & Updates page](/corrections.html).

## Before you write

For a planning question, you may get a faster starting point from the [Solar System Planner](/system-planner.html), [cost guides](/guides.html), or [Project Lab](/diy-off-grid-energy.html).
