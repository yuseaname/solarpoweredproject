## FAQ
{{< faq "How much does a small off-grid solar system cost?" >}}
A small cabin or RV setup — 400W of panel, 1–2 kWh of storage, and a 1000–2000W inverter — typically lands between $800 and $2,500 in parts when you buy components separately.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Is DIY solar cheaper than an installed system?" >}}
Material-for-material, yes — often 40–60% less, because installed quotes bundle labor, permitting, and margin. You are trading that saving for your own design, code, and warranty work.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "What is the biggest cost in a solar system?" >}}
For grid-tied installs, labor and soft costs. For off-grid builds, storage: batteries routinely cost more than the panels they support, which is why battery math dominates DIY budgets.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "How much does one 100W solar panel cost?" >}}
Roughly $0.80–$1.20 per watt for a reputable 100W module — around $80–$120. Price per watt usually drops as panel size rises, which is why larger modules dominate rooftop installs.
{{< /faq >}}
