## Formula

**Daily Wh** = Panel watts × Peak sun hours × System efficiency

Example: 400W × 4.5 hours × 0.8 ≈ 1,440Wh/day

Use peak sun hours for your location (not daylight hours). Pair this estimate with your <a href="solar-system-sizing.html" class="text-link">daily load plan</a> when sizing batteries.

## Choose an efficiency factor

Use 0.75 to 0.85 to account for losses from heat, wiring, and inverter efficiency.

Losses also come from dust and shading. If your output is lower than expected, review <a href="solar-output-troubleshooting.html" class="text-link">low output troubleshooting</a> and <a href="solar-panel-cleaning-cost.html" class="text-link">panel cleaning basics</a>.
