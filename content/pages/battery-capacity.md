## Formula

**Battery Wh** = Daily Wh × Days of autonomy ÷ Depth of discharge

Example: 2,000Wh × 1 day ÷ 0.8 = 2,500Wh

## Tips

For solar systems, usable capacity matters more than nameplate. Match your battery bank to <a href="how-to-choose-solar-system-voltage.html" class="text-link">system voltage</a> and <a href="solar-inverter-sizing.html" class="text-link">inverter draw</a> so cabling and protection stay realistic.

-   Li-ion often supports deeper discharge than lead-acid.
-   Factor in inverter efficiency for AC loads.
-   Size for critical loads first.
-   Cold temperatures reduce usable capacity.
