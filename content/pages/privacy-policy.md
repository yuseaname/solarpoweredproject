## Data we collect

We collect standard usage data such as pages visited, time on page, browser type, device type, and approximate location. This helps us understand which guides are most useful and improve site performance.

## Analytics

We use privacy-focused analytics (currently Rybbit) to measure traffic trends. Analytics providers may process IP addresses and device data for aggregated reporting, fraud prevention, and system security.

## Advertising and cookies

This site may use Google AdSense and other advertising partners. These vendors use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, and limit repeated ads. Google may use cookies to personalize ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

Third-party vendors may also use cookies or device identifiers for ad measurement and personalization, subject to your region and consent settings. Learn more about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps: <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites" class="text-link">policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites</a>.

You can control ad personalization in your Google Ads settings: <a href="https://adssettings.google.com/" class="text-link">adssettings.google.com</a>.

## Your choices

You can limit cookies through your browser settings and clear existing cookies at any time. Blocking cookies may affect how ads are served and how usage is measured.
