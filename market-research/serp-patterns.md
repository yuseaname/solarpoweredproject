## Synthesis by class

**Commercial roundups.** No single type dominates all four. "Best solar generator for home backup" is retailer product pages + CNN Underscored (tested). "Best 100W panel" is tested publishers (SolarReviews, OutdoorGearLab) + Home Depot. But "best MPPT charge controller 2026" is **mostly small independent affiliate blogs** (four in the top 10) plus Reddit — the tested-publisher lock is absent. **INFERENCE:** roundup winnability is product-size-dependent: big-ticket home-backup units attract tested media money; component-level roundups (charge controllers) are still winnable by small sites. Reddit threads appear in all four — UGC is being used as a quality check on commercial intent.

**Comparisons.** Forum-dominated across all four (MPPT vs PWM: 7 Reddit threads in top 10; Jackery vs EcoFlow: 9). **INFERENCE:** Google has no editorial result it trusts for these head-to-heads — most comparison content is brand-written or affiliate-thin. A math-first, no-affiliate-bias comparison (e.g., $/usable-Wh over cycle life for LiFePO4 vs lead acid, with a break-even calculator) is the strongest editorial gap in the niche. Manufacturer pages (Morningstar, SolaX) rank but are spec-sheet-flavored, not decision math.

**Troubleshooting.** The starkest pattern: "solar battery not charging" is 9/10 Reddit; "why is my inverter shutting off" is forums + Quora + TikTok + YouTube with **zero dedicated editorial guides** in the top 10. **INFERENCE:** this matches the client's own proven winners (MPPT-not-charging, fuse sizing pages get the site's pageviews). Symptom→cause→fix pages with real electrical math (LVD thresholds, voltage drop, fuse sizing per NEC) face almost no editorial competition. Lead with this class.

**Cost.** National cost queries are locked by EnergySage (marketplace with proprietary transaction data) and ConsumerAffairs; battery cost adds installer pages (solar.com, nationprousa.com) and a wall of YouTube. Local (CA) is EnergySage's local-data page plus three installers. **INFERENCE:** a small site without proprietary pricing data cannot credibly compete on cost pages; deprioritize both, or reframe cost content around *sizing math* (what size system your load requires) rather than price claims.

**Tools/calculators.** Interactive tools dominate both queries, and **small independent sites rank at #1–2** (explorist.life, freesunpower.com, sunsolartilt.com, professionalcalculators.com, baess.app). The client already ships 5 inline calculators. **INFERENCE:** best structural fit — tool + NEC-cited worked example is a page type the incumbents do poorly (freesunpower is a dated PHP tool; several are ad-heavy shells).

**Appliance runtime.** "What size solar generator to run a refrigerator" is brand-blog-dominated (ALLPOWERS, EcoFlow, Jackery, Anker Solix — all selling the answer). "100Ah fridge runtime" is more open: niche forums plus one small tool page (offgridlivingtools.com). CPAP is retailer (cpap.com, sleeplay.com) + Reddit + YouTube. **INFERENCE:** runtime pages win when they show the duty-cycle math (compressor start surge, average draw, DoD) instead of recommending a SKU; the 100Ah-fridge and CPAP-runtime angles are enterable, the brand-SKU-framed "what size generator" query is not.

**Local.** See Cost — installer/marketplace lock. Deprioritize.

**AI Overviews / snippets.** Not directly observable via Brave. **INFERENCE:** heavy-Reddit SERPs (troubleshooting, comparisons) typically correlate with AI Overview presence in Google; definitional queries (MPPT vs PWM) are featured-snippet-prone. Treat AI Overviews as a CTR risk on informational queries; troubleshooting pages can still win the click by offering the *complete* decision tree the overview summarizes.

## Ranked winnability (small, honest, math-first affiliate site)

1. **Troubleshooting** (solar battery not charging; inverter shutting off) — 80–90% forum/UGC SERPs, zero editorial competition, matches the site's only proven traffic winners. Page type: symptom→cause→fix guide + calculator embed.
2. **Tools/calculators** (wire size, tilt angle) — small tools already rank #1–2; site has calculator infrastructure. Page type: calculator + worked NEC-referenced example.
3. **Comparisons** (MPPT vs PWM; LiFePO4 vs lead acid) — forum-dominated, no tested-publisher lock. Page type: break-even math comparison, no "best" claims, no SKU bias.
4. **Runtime — 100Ah fridge / CPAP runtime** — forums + weak brand math; winnable with duty-cycle calculators. Avoid the brand-locked "what size generator" framing.
5. **Commercial — component roundups only** (best MPPT controller) — small affiliates demonstrably rank; enter only with a transparent, disclosed methodology. Skip "best solar generator for home backup" and "best 100W panel" (CNN/OutdoorGearLab/SolarReviews lock).
6. **Cost (national)** — EnergySage/ConsumerAffairs data lock; only viable as sizing-math reframing.
7. **Local cost (CA)** — most locked class; deprioritize entirely.

**Recurring domains to beat:** reddit.com (17/18 SERPs), YouTube (13/18), energysage.com (all cost/local), brand blogs (EcoFlow/Jackery/Anker/ALLPOWERS in runtime + lease-vs-buy). **Proof small sites rank:** explorist.life, ondemandinstantsolar.com, sunforgelab.com, offgridlivingtools.com, baess.app, poweroutage.us — all independent, all in captured top-10s.
